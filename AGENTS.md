## Website architecture
- Keep shared corporate navigation and footer in a reusable site shell, with distinct content routes and page-specific metadata so each page is independently shareable.
- Keep contact enquiries linked to the company's existing hosted contact form until a delivery service is connected; never simulate successful enquiry submission.
- Treat generated engineering photography as illustrative imagery, never as evidence of completed client projects.
